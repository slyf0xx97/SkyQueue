Autonomous SG 2026 · Airbus Challenge #2 · The solution

AI suggests. Code decides.
A person approves.

Metering decides when aircraft leave the gate. SkyQueue settles the trade-offs it can't:

Problem · Queue waits with engines running

Agents agree who waits at the gate, engines off, even when crews or gates complicate it.

2.7 t less fuel a storm hour*

Problem · Exceptions settled by phone, one flight at a time

Tower, Pilot and Ground agents negotiate up to 3 rounds and send one plan to approve.

Fewer calls, one approval

Problem · Rushed re-ordering risks spacing and crew limits

Plain code tests spacing, fuel, crew duty and gates before a person approves.

0 unsafe plans approved

* Estimate: 30 flights each held 10 min at the gate with engines off, ≈ 8.5 t less CO₂.
Every decision links to its reason, and we measure all three against today's order on real 2015 flights.

The way
forward.

Afternoon thunderstorms are routine at tropical hubs. The storm queue is a Southeast Asian problem too.

01 · Shadow mode at a home hub

Live data, advisory only, at a hub such as Singapore Changi. Compare our plans with what the tower did.

02 · Live advisory

Supervisor approves. Plug into A-CDM, AODB and ACARS. One Pilot agent per airline.

03 · Regional rollout

More ASEAN hubs. Each airport's minima become rule parameters. Same code, same log.
